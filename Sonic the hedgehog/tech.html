Experience the Adventure

Sonic the Hedgehog is a video game franchise created and produced by Sega. The franchise centers on Sonic, an anthropomorphic blue hedgehog who battles the evil Doctor Eggman, a mad scientist. The main Sonic the Hedgehog games are platformers developed by Sonic Team; other games, developed by various studios, include spin-offs in the racing, fighting, party, and sports genres.

Different Bosses

Sonic the Hedgehog has had many bosses, including Dr. Eggman, Shadow the Hedgehog, and Knuckles the Echidna etc. Each boss has their own unique abilities and personalities. Sonic must defeat these bosses in order to save the world from destruction.
